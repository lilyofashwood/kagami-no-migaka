The chosen title is **Kagami-no-Migaka**, with **かがみのしきしのみがか** as its companion poetry and description. The earlier **かのとこよ · 歌ノ常世** title now lives in the README’s poems.

## Two faces of the night-song

**歌ノ常世（かのとこよ） ⇄ 夜言ノ香（よことのか）**

The kana reverse exactly into one another. “The song’s eternal realm” and “fragrance of night words” are the chosen glosses for these deliberately coined faces. **歌=か** is an intentional reading. Kana provides the reversal; each side receives its own kanji image. [常世](https://www.kanjipedia.jp/kotoba/0003510000) brings the enduring otherworld into the song.

The source address is [lilyofashwood/kagami-no-migaka](https://github.com/lilyofashwood/kagami-no-migaka). The Roman slug identifies the project; the reversible lettering lives in its kana poems. Protocol IDs and the local directory remain unchanged.
